# Frohlich Lab — Ecology, Evolution, and Environmental Change

Welcome to the **Frohlich Lab** repository — the digital home of our research group at the **Institute of Nature Conservation, Polish Academy of Sciences**.

Our lab explores how animals adapt to an ever-changing world, integrating ecology, evolution, and conservation through data-driven science and global collaboration.

🌍 **Main research areas:**
- **Climate and evolution** — how environmental regimes shape morphology, physiology, and adaptation.  
- **Senses and perception** — how sensory systems evolve across sound, light, and odour environments.  
- **The living deadwood** — how saproxylic systems sustain biodiversity and ecosystem resilience.  
- **Urban nature** — how cities can balance safety, aesthetics, and conservation.  
- **Causal networks** — uncovering hidden pathways linking environment, traits, and behavior.
